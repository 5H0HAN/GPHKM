SCOPE: Product-wide web redesign led by the Operate surfaces: customer marketplace and workspace, staff operations console, and the public introduction page. Audience: customer organization owners and members, Admin, Finance, and Super Admin. Job: discover placements, place and track orders, and manage catalog/fulfillment/money safely. Success: tasks and status become clear at a glance on desktop and mobile. Preserve product behavior, permissions, supplier confidentiality, the immutable financial model, dark-by-default preference, user-selectable light mode, English, and responsive WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not invent proof, promises, or a public brand identity.

THESIS: GPHKM is a placement production desk: customers choose a publication opportunity from a clear flatplan; each order item becomes a proof record that moves through review, partner handoff, and publication check. It refuses generic SaaS dashboards that scatter one production flow across unrelated cards.

OWN-WORLD: Magazine flatplans, proof sheets, folio rules, and restrained correction marks. Use a deep ink ground by default, warm paper-toned content surfaces and type, coral correction accents for primary action and review, and sage for approved/complete states; light mode reverses the ink and paper roles without losing contrast. Use folio-aligned grids, strong editorial title hierarchy, readable sans UI labels, tabular figures, crisp rules, and restrained square/rounded edges. Product labels and controls remain literal. No ornamental paper texture or fabricated editorial endorsement.

STORY: Customers understand what a placement includes, what they must provide, what each item costs, and which customer-safe milestone comes next. Staff see prioritized queues, immutable item history, evidence, and role-appropriate actions. Supplier identity/source classification, customer-internal sourcing, and finance-only data remain behind existing server-side permissions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The customer marketplace opens with organization context and a compact persistent workspace navigation. The content area starts with the page title, a clear search/filter rule, then an adaptable listing index with prominent price, visible URL access state, key terms, and available source-attributed metrics. A compact selected-order tray leads to a separate three-step flow: choose placements, enter details per placement, and review prices, content policy, and sharing consent before placing the order. The tray remains keyboard-operable and must not cover listing content. On mobile the shell becomes a compact top bar, the index becomes one readable column, and order actions stack into full-width controls. Staff pages use the same folio grid for queues and evidence, not customer cards pasted into an admin shell.

FORM: The user selected the top-ranked grounded direction, Editorial production flatplan, from the direction round (seed key 90298254), with Code first. Its proof-sheet grid, folio rules, and correction marks are carried across customer, staff, and public surfaces; its editorial appearance never changes literal labels, workflow, roles, or claims.
