# Record supplier payments as transfer events

**Status:** Accepted

Each Finance or Super Admin record represents an actual manual supplier transfer and is append-only; partial payments are allowed. Recording a transfer requires a private PDF or EML receipt (up to 5 MiB) and a separate transaction/evidence reference. The receipt is hash-checked on download, private, attachment-only, and downloadable only by Finance/Super Admin with an audit event. One transfer may be allocated across multiple order items for that supplier. Supplier-sourced listings must use the same supplier for sourcing and fulfillment; platform-owned listings may name one external fulfillment supplier. Allocation amounts must exactly equal the recorded transfer amount, and duplicate order-item allocations within one transfer are rejected. Transfer references must be unique per supplier (case-insensitive after trimming), so duplicate references fail closed. Unpaid, partially paid, paid, and overpaid are derived by comparing cumulative allocations with confirmed actual costs attributed to the same supplier and item; unknown costs remain provisional and overpayments are flagged for review. The receipt is evidence supplied by staff; GPHKM does not independently verify bank settlement or initiate supplier payouts. This replaces the ambiguous paid/unpaid status-row model and prevents a status entry from being mistaken for a money movement.

Existing PAID/UNPAID rows remain immutable legacy evidence. They are visible to Finance but are not converted into transfer events or included in payable reconciliation, because the old records do not reliably establish that money moved.
